★About Me ★

I may be a literal grill on the internet, but my name is Borealis Capps in the offline world. I'm an award-winning poet and author living with my three cats and partner in the Pacific Northwest. I once ran Rulebook Review, a service dedicated to editing and reviewing board game rulebooks where I got to work with amazing companies like IDW Comics and more. I wrote for PokeGoldfish when it existed helping brewing budget decks for the Pokémon TCG as well as talking about meta trends. I’ve puttered around various esports scenes and love digging into obscure board games and dead card games. In short: I’m a big ol nerd for gaming!

I’m quite a performer, having been in musicals but more relevantly spending four years working for a circus. I love seeing people’s eyes light up when they see something truly remarkable and want to bring that to tables I play at whenever possible. I’ve combined this with being a punk that loves visible mending and using things to death to try and make some truly unique gaming accessories. I hope to use recycled materials to help folks on a budget have something that still looks sick as hell at the game table while trying to cut back on junk clogging up landfills.
