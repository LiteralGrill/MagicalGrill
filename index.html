By the Power of Charcoal!!!

Step into the sizzling circle where spells and steaks collide! I'm here serve up hot takes on Magic: The Gathering and maybe other tabletop games on occation with a side of sparkle, sass, and searing strategy. Watching my transformation sequence is not optional!

You'll come to learn that I build my decks with a flair for the dramatic and feel like every game of commander can be made better with some theatrical performance. I'm a big fan of making themed deck boxes to boggle the mind and make game nights all the more magical. I'll also share simple plans to make deck boxes and other accessories literal garbage you might have around the house. Hopefully, I can help everyone refuse, reduce, redistribute, reuse, and recycle whenever possible to help offset all that paper and plastic used in our hobby.

You can see a sampling of my most recent works below and check out the full breadth of my work in the archive. I just got everything set up here so there would be a clean split between my writing on more adult subjects, so have patience! More articles will be here soon~
